Children of Time

by Neil.B.Ennis 2 October 2026 Shortlink | |

Title: Children of Time
Author: Adrian Tchaikovsky
Published: 2015
Pages: 600
Structure: 65 chapters in 8 parts

What happens when civilisations collide, and the remnants of a dying people find a perfect world that is already inhabited? Who deserves the spoils, and how could there possibly be a middle way?

Children of Time is a gripping tale of humanity’s desperate attempt to survive after centuries of conflict render Earth a wasteland. It’s also the grand story of the birth of a new civilisation as it evolves from simplicity to elegant and intelligent complexity.
